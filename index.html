Privacy Policy

Home Construction Guide

Last updated: 10 June 2026

Home Construction Guide is a simple, offline reference app that presents a step-by-step home construction guide. Your privacy matters to us, and this policy explains how the app handles data — in short, it doesn't collect any.

The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It works entirely on your device and offline.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, gather, or process any personal or sensitive information. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no profiles.

How the app works

  • All content is bundled inside the app and shown directly on your device.
  • The app does not send any data to us or to any server.
  • The app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party data-collection tools.
  • The app does not require any permissions that access your personal data.

Data sharing

Because the app collects no data, there is nothing to share, sell, or disclose to any third party.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for a general audience and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach the developer using the contact details listed on the app's Google Play store listing.
